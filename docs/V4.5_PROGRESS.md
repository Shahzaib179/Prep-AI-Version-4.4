# Prep AI v4.5 - habits and smarter practice

## New for students
* **Today page** (sidebar, under Dashboard): daily goal ring, 7-day activity chart, streak, level and XP bar, today's revision queue and the next best action.
  It reads only stored data, so it works even when the AI service is rate-limited.
* **Streaks**: practise at least once a day. A streak stays alive until midnight, so "active yesterday, nothing yet today" shows a warning instead of resetting.
  Day boundaries follow `PROGRESS_TZ_OFFSET_MIN` in `config.py` (default 300 = Pakistan, UTC+5).
* **Daily goal** (default 20 questions, changeable 5-200) and **XP / levels**: 2 XP per answered question, +3 per correct, +10 per finished quiz, +20 for a perfect score.
  Level n starts at 50 x (n-1)^2 XP. Milestone achievements: 3/7/14/30-day streaks, level-ups, daily goal reached.
* **Retake my mistakes**: wrong answers from now on are stored with their options, so they come back as real MCQs (no AI needed) until answered correctly.
  Mistakes made before v4.5 have no stored options and are skipped.
* **No repeated questions**: every question a student has seen is remembered (hashed). New quizzes avoid them; the AI is told what to avoid and asked once more if too many repeats come back.
  If a topic is small and everything has been seen, the quiz is topped up with repeats (fresh ones first) rather than shrinking.
